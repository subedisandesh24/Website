About Me

I am Sandesh Subedi, son of Rishiram Subedi and Sabitri Devi Subedi, born on 25 April 2001 in Syangja, Nepal. Growing up in a rural agricultural community, I witnessed the resilience of farmers, which inspired me to develop innovative solutions for the agricultural sector.

Currently pursuing an M.S. in Horticulture, my interests focus on artificial intelligence (AI), digital agriculture, and computer vision. I am passionate about applying image analysis for disease detection and yield estimation, bridging the gap between traditional practices and digital technology.
