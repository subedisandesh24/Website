About Me

I am Sandesh Subedi, son of Rishiram Subedi and Sabitri Devi Subedi, born on 25 April 2001 (2058/01/12 B.S.) in Syangja, Nepal. Growing up in a rural agricultural community, I witnessed the dedication, hard work, and resilience of farmers in my village. Observing how agriculture shaped the livelihood of people around me inspired me to develop a strong interest in farming, agricultural development, and innovative solutions for the agricultural sector.

I completed my school-level education at Shishu Niketan Secondary School. I am a graduate of B.Sc. Agriculture from Tribhuvan University and currently pursuing an M.S. in Horticulture at Agriculture and Forestry University.

My academic and professional interests focus on agriculture, artificial intelligence (AI), digital agriculture, research, and technology-driven innovation. I am particularly interested in the application of AI, computer vision, machine learning, and image analysis in agriculture, especially in areas such as crop monitoring, disease detection, yield estimation, precision agriculture, and smart farming systems.

My research and professional experiences include work in agricultural research, image-based data analysis, computer vision applications, tomato crop studies, digital agriculture, and AI-driven agricultural tools. I am passionate about integrating modern technologies with agricultural science to address practical challenges faced by farmers and agricultural communities.

I aspire to contribute to the advancement of AI-powered agriculture, horticultural innovation, and sustainable agricultural development, helping bridge the gap between traditional farming practices and emerging digital technologies in Nepal and beyond.

🍅 Tomato Research (AI-Based Tomato Monitoring System)

My tomato research focuses on developing an AI-powered smart monitoring system for tomato crop growth, yield estimation, and disease detection using computer vision and deep learning techniques. The system integrates multiple YOLO-based models to analyze tomato plants from images and videos under real field conditions.

The research includes:

  • Automated tomato fruit detection and counting using deep learning models
  • Classification of tomatoes into red, turning, and green stages for maturity analysis
  • Video-based crop monitoring for continuous field assessment
  • Leaf disease detection and classification, including major tomato diseases such as early blight, late blight, bacterial spot, powdery mildew, leaf mold, and viral infections
  • A decision-support system that recommends appropriate plant protection measures based on detected diseases
  • Yield estimation using image-based weight approximation, where fruit size is converted into weight using reference scaling techniques

This system is designed to reduce manual labor, improve accuracy in field data collection, and support precision agriculture practices for tomato production in Nepal. It also enables real-time monitoring of crop health and productivity, helping farmers and researchers make faster and more informed decisions.

Overall, this research bridges agriculture and artificial intelligence by applying computer vision for practical crop monitoring, disease management, and yield prediction.

🐝 Bee Research (Hive Health & Pollinator Monitoring System)

My bee research focuses on developing an AI-based hive health and pollinator monitoring system that combines computer vision and deep learning to identify, classify, and track bees and their ecosystem interactions in agricultural environments. The system is designed to support pollination research and hive management through automated detection and species-level identification of bees.

Key components of the research include:

  • Bee detection system using YOLO-based object detection models to identify bees in images and field visuals
  • Species identification and classification of major honeybee species found in Nepal, including:
    • - Apis laboriosa (Himalayan Giant Honey Bee)
    • - Apis dorsata (Giant Honey Bee)
    • - Apis cerana (Asian Hive Bee)
    • - Apis florea (Little Honey Bee)
    • - Apis mellifera (European Honey Bee)
  • A knowledge-based species information system, providing ecological and agricultural details about each detected bee species
  • Pollinator monitoring framework for studying bee presence, distribution, and activity patterns in agricultural fields
  • Integration of AI tools for supporting bee health monitoring and biodiversity assessment

This research contributes to understanding the role of pollinators in agriculture and supports data-driven pollination management strategies. The system helps bridge entomology, ecology, and artificial intelligence, enabling automated monitoring of pollinator populations and their impact on agricultural productivity.
