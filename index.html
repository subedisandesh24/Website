About Me

I am Sandesh Subedi, son of Rishiram Subedi and Sabitri Devi Subedi, born on 25 April 2001 (2058/01/12 B.S.) in Syangja, Nepal. Growing up in a rural agricultural community, I witnessed the dedication, hard work, and resilience of farmers in my village. Observing how agriculture shaped the livelihood of people around me inspired me to develop a strong interest in farming, agricultural development, and innovative solutions for the agricultural sector.

I completed my school-level education at Shishu Niketan Secondary School. I am a graduate of B.Sc. Agriculture from Tribhuvan University and currently pursuing an M.S. in Horticulture at Agriculture and Forestry University.

My academic and professional interests focus on agriculture, artificial intelligence (AI), digital agriculture, research, and technology-driven innovation. I am particularly interested in the application of AI, computer vision, machine learning, and image analysis in agriculture, especially in areas such as crop monitoring, disease detection, yield estimation, precision agriculture, and smart farming systems.

My research and professional experiences include work in agricultural research, image-based data analysis, computer vision applications, tomato crop studies, digital agriculture, and AI-driven agricultural tools. I am passionate about integrating modern technologies with agricultural science to address practical challenges faced by farmers and agricultural communities.

I aspire to contribute to the advancement of AI-powered agriculture, horticultural innovation, and sustainable agricultural development, helping bridge the gap between traditional farming practices and emerging digital technologies in Nepal and beyond.

Web Applications & AI Research

🍅 Tomato Research (AI-Based Tomato Monitoring System)

My tomato research focuses on developing an AI-powered smart monitoring system for tomato crop growth, yield estimation, and disease detection using computer vision and deep learning techniques. The system integrates multiple YOLO-based models to analyze tomato plants from images and videos under real field conditions.

  • Automated tomato fruit detection and counting using deep learning models
  • Classification of tomatoes into red, turning, and green stages for maturity analysis
  • Leaf disease detection: Early blight, late blight, bacterial spot, powdery mildew, and viral infections
  • Yield estimation using image-based weight approximation with reference scaling techniques
Launch Tomato App

🐝 Bee Research (Hive Health & Pollinator Monitoring System)

Developing an AI-based hive health and pollinator monitoring system that combines computer vision and deep learning to identify, classify, and track bees and their ecosystem interactions. Designed to support pollination research through automated detection and species-level identification.

  • Identification of major species in Nepal: Apis laboriosa, Apis dorsata, Apis cerana, Apis florea, and Apis mellifera.
  • Pollinator monitoring framework for studying distribution and activity patterns
  • Integration of AI tools for biodiversity assessment and data-driven management
