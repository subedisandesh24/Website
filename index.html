About Me

I am Sandesh Subedi, son of Rishiram Subedi and Sabitri Devi Subedi, born on 25 April 2001 in Syangja, Nepal. Growing up in a rural agricultural community, I witnessed the dedication and resilience of farmers, which inspired me to pursue **innovative solutions for the agricultural sector**.

Currently pursuing an **M.S. in Horticulture**, my interests lie at the intersection of **Agriculture and Artificial Intelligence**. I am passionate about applying **Computer Vision, Machine Learning, and Image Analysis** to practical challenges like crop monitoring, disease detection, and yield estimation to bridge the gap between traditional farming and digital technology.
