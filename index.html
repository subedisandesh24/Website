About Me

I am Sandesh Subedi, son of Rishiram Subedi and Sabitri Devi Subedi, born on 25 April 2001 in Syangja, Nepal. Growing up in a rural agricultural community inspired me to develop a strong interest in farming and innovative solutions for the agricultural sector.

My academic and professional interests focus on artificial intelligence (AI), digital agriculture, and technology-driven innovation. I specialize in applying computer vision and machine learning for crop monitoring, disease detection, and yield estimation, bridging the gap between traditional farming and modern technology.
